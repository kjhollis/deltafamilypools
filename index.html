Common Questions

My pool has turned green. Can it be saved without draining it? In most cases, yes. A professional algae cleanup treats and kills the algae, then brushes, vacuums and rebalances the water over a few visits. Draining is usually only needed in severe cases.

How much does weekly pool service cost? It depends on pool size, condition and how much equipment care is included. A professional can give you an exact monthly price once they know your pool — call and describe it.

I think my pool is leaking. How is a leak found? A leak detection visit typically starts with checking the equipment and plumbing, then uses pressure testing and dye testing to isolate where the water is escaping before any repair is quoted.

My pump stopped working. Should I repair or replace it? It depends on the pump's age and what failed — motors, seals and controls can often be repaired, while an older, inefficient pump may be cheaper to replace. A professional will talk you through both options.
